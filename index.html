Installation

How to Install

1
Download the APK

Tap Download APK above. If you're on a PC or phone, transfer it to your TV via USB or the Send Files to TV app.

2
Enable Unknown Sources on your TV

Go to Settings → Device Preferences → Security and turn on Unknown Sources (or Install unknown apps).

3
Transfer the APK to your TV

Use a USB drive, Send Files to TV app, or sideload via adb install SportlinkTV.apk over your local network.

4
Install & Launch

Open the APK file from your file manager on the TV, tap Install, then open Sportlink TV from your app drawer.
